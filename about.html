ABOUT

Sound tools by Invisible.

Creative Sound is a small studio with one focus: plugins for people who'd rather bend a sound out of shape than leave it alone.

Why Creative Sound exists

Most processing plugins are built to be transparent — to get out of the way. Creative Sound builds the opposite kind: tools designed to be heard, to add character on purpose, for sound designers, mixers and producers who want their chain to sound like them, not like a preset.

Invisible

Creative Sound is built by Invisible — one person handling design, DSP and code, starting with Creative Dist, a modular distortion engine born out of wanting a saturator that didn't just add warmth, but let you reorder, reshape and break the signal on your own terms.

What's next

Creative Dist is the first plugin, not the last. More tools are in development — if you want to know when something new ships, the best way is to get in touch.
