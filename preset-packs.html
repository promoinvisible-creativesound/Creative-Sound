Free Serum presets for producers

Creative Sound makes free Serum and Serum 2 preset packs. Creative Presets has 150 presets across bass, lead, chords, FX, reese and pluck, plus 70 VIP presets from seven guest sound designers. Creative Presets Vol. 2 adds 82 more across bass, lead, chord, pads and stab.

Listen to each pack in the browser before you download. Every preset is royalty-free, so you can use it in your own releases.

Are these Serum presets really free?

Yes. Both packs are free to download. You enter your email at checkout, no payment is needed, and the download link is sent to that email.

Do they work in Serum 2 as well as Serum?

Creative Presets is made for Serum and Serum 2, and Creative Presets Vol. 2 is a Serum preset pack. Each pack page lists exactly what it contains.

Can I use the presets in my own music?

Yes. All presets are 100% royalty-free, so you can use them in commercial releases without paying anything back.

How do I install Serum presets?

Copy the preset files into your Serum presets folder (Documents/Xfer/Serum Presets/Presets for Serum, Documents/Xfer/Serum 2 Presets/Presets for Serum 2), then reload the preset list in the plugin.

Do the packs include samples or construction kits?

No. These are synth presets only, with no samples or construction kits.

Where do I find my download again?

The link comes by email. If you create an account with the same email, your packs are listed in your profile so you can download them again.
