Privacy Policy

Last updated: 28 October 2026

This policy explains what personal data Creative Sound collects through creativesound.io, the Creative Dist 2.0 purchase flow, the free sound packs, the track store and the producer area, why we collect it, who else processes it and what rights you have.

Who we are

The data controller is Emanuele Paparella (known as Invisible), trading as Creative Sound, based at Via G. Manzù 36, 00013 Mentana (RM), Italy, VAT number (P.IVA) 17024071007. Creative Sound is the trade name of this business, not a separate company. For any privacy question or request, write to hello@creativesound.io or, by certified email (PEC), to emanuelepaparella65@pec.it.

What we collect, and why

  • Account: your email address, a securely hashed password (we never store the password itself), and, if you add them, your first and last name. We use them to let you sign in, show your licenses and packs, and send account emails such as the verification code and password reset links. Legal basis: performance of a contract (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR).
  • Purchases, tracks and free packs: checkout is handled by Stripe. We receive your email address and basic order data (product, amount, currency, date, Stripe session ID). For Creative Dist 2.0 we generate a license key linked to your email. We never see or store your card details. When you buy a track, we also record which track and license you chose and a license ID, and we put your name (as entered at checkout) and email in the license agreement PDF we send you. Legal basis: contract; and legal obligation (Art. 6(1)(c)) for keeping accounting records.
  • Support tickets: the subject and message you send from your account, linked to your email, so we can answer you. Legal basis: contract.
  • "Notify me" sign-ups: if you ask to be told about an upcoming release (for example the sample packs), we store your email address, the topic and the date, and use them only to send that one announcement. Legal basis: your consent (Art. 6(1)(a)), which you can withdraw at any time.
  • Job applications ("Work with us"): your name, email, area of interest, message and, if attached, your CV are sent to our inbox by email. They are not stored on the website. Legal basis: steps taken at your request before a possible contract (Art. 6(1)(b)).
  • Public profile and storefront: if you add them, your username, display name, bio, photo, social links, genres, an optional contact email and the tracks you sell. These are shown publicly on your profile page by design, so only add what you want to be public. Legal basis: contract; for the optional contact email, your consent (Art. 6(1)(a)).
  • Producers: tracks, tax details and payouts: the tracks you upload (cover, preview, audio files, title, prices, license settings and your producer tag); and, so that we can pay you, your legal name, country, address, tax ID and VAT number, date of birth (individuals), a record of your confirmation with its date, time and IP address, your PayPal email or Stripe account reference, and a record of your sales, our fee and your payouts. Legal basis: contract; and legal obligation (Art. 6(1)(c)) for tax and accounting records.
  • Messages between members: the messages you send and receive about tracks. The other person sees your name, username and photo, never your email. Legal basis: contract.
  • Saved tracks and listening figures: tracks you save with the heart are linked to your account. Plays and downloads of a track are counted without identifying you and shown to the producer as totals. Legal basis: contract; and legitimate interest (Art. 6(1)(f)) for the counts.
  • Suggestions in the Track Store: to show "Picked for you", the store keeps a small taste profile made of the genres, moods, tags, tempos and keys of the tracks you play, save or add to your cart, with older activity counting less. It is not built from your name or email and is not shared with producers or anyone else. It stays in your browser; when you are signed in, a copy is also kept in your account so it follows you across devices. The default order of the store is a random mix that gives every producer a fair turn and does not depend on you. Legal basis: legitimate interest (Art. 6(1)(f)) in showing relevant tracks. You can switch it off by clearing this site's data in your browser and by writing to us to delete the copy in your account.
  • Technical data: our hosting provider keeps standard server logs (such as IP address, browser type and requested page) for security and reliability. Legal basis: legitimate interest (Art. 6(1)(f)) in keeping the site secure.

Browsing the site, listening to previews and downloading the free demo do not require an account. Downloading a free pack or buying a license does, so that your purchase is tied to your profile.

Cookies and similar technologies

  • Login cookie: when you sign in, we set one cookie that keeps you logged in (for up to 30 days, or until you log out). It's needed for your account to work and isn't used for tracking.
  • Session storage: the site remembers in your browser tab that the intro animation has already played, so it isn't repeated on every page. Nothing that identifies you.
  • Local storage: your track cart, hearted tracks, the taste profile behind suggestions and a few display choices are kept in your browser. They stay on your device, except saved tracks and the taste profile, which are also stored in your account when you are signed in. The tab's session storage also holds a random number that sets the order of the Track Store for that visit.
  • Analytics: we use Vercel Web Analytics, which does not use cookies and reports aggregated figures (page views, country, device type, and clicks on key buttons such as Buy, Free demo and Download). It does not identify you or follow you across other websites.

We don't use advertising cookies, tracking pixels or social media plugins.

Who else processes your data

We rely on a small number of providers, each acting on our behalf or as an independent controller for their own service:

  • Stripe: payments and checkout and, for producers who choose a bank payout, Stripe Connect, where Stripe verifies your identity under its own terms. See Stripe's Privacy Policy.
  • Neon: hosts our database (accounts, profiles, licenses, sales and payouts, messages, pack downloads, tickets, sign-ups). See Neon's Privacy Policy.
  • Resend: sends our emails (verification codes, license keys, download links, password resets). See Resend's Privacy Policy.
  • Vercel: hosts the website, runs its server functions and provides the analytics described above. See Vercel's Privacy Policy.
  • Dropbox: hosts the sound pack files you download and the audio files, covers and previews of tracks. See Dropbox's Privacy Policy.
  • PayPal: sends payouts to producers who choose PayPal. See PayPal's Privacy Statement.
  • jsDelivr: delivers one audio-encoding script, only when a producer prepares a producer tag, and can see your IP address when your browser loads it. See jsDelivr's privacy policy. The site's own typefaces and animation scripts are served from creativesound.io.

Some of these providers are based in, or process data in, the United States or other countries outside the EU/EEA. In those cases data is transferred under the EU–US Data Privacy Framework or the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses. We never sell your data.

How long we keep it

  • Account: until you ask us to delete it.
  • Licenses and purchase records: for as long as your license is valid, and in any case for the 10 years required by Italian accounting law.
  • Verification codes and password reset links: they expire after 15 minutes and 1 hour respectively.
  • Tax details and payout records: 10 years after the last payment, as required by Italian accounting and tax law.
  • Profile, tracks and messages: until you delete them or ask us to delete your account. A license that was already sold stays valid.
  • Support tickets: 24 months after the ticket is closed.
  • "Notify me" sign-ups: deleted once the announcement has been sent, or earlier if you ask.
  • Job applications: 12 months, unless you ask us to delete them sooner.
  • Server logs: according to Vercel's standard retention.

Your rights

Under the GDPR you can ask to access, correct, delete or export your data, to restrict or object to its processing, and to withdraw your consent at any time (this doesn't affect what happened before). Write to hello@creativesound.io; we'll answer within one month. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with the Italian data protection authority, the Garante per la protezione dei dati personali, or with the authority of the EU country where you live.

Children

The site and the account area are not meant for children under 14. If you believe a child has given us personal data, contact us and we'll delete it.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update the date at the top of this page. If the change is significant and you have an account, we'll also let you know by email.
