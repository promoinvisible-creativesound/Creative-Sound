Your licenses

Set the limits of each license and add terms of your own. The PDF every buyer gets is built from these. Changes apply to new sales only; past buyers keep the license they bought.

Your own terms

Your terms are added to every license and shown to artists before they buy. They can add obligations (a release link, a tag in the title) but cannot take away what the standard contract grants.

Always included: the standard contract
  • Non-exclusive license for one new song, worldwide, not transferable
  • Credit as "" wherever credits are shown
  • You keep the copyright of the beat; the artist owns their vocals and master
  • No Content ID registration of the beat; you clear claims on request
  • Films, TV, ads and games need a separate agreement with you
  • No resale or reuse of the files, no sampling into new beats, no AI training
  • 30 days to fix a breach, perpetual for the song, Italian law
