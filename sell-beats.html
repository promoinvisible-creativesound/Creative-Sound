Your own storefront

Previews with your tag

Previews carry your producer tag, so buyers hear your name. Pick the tag in your producer area and play every track in place.

Paid every week

Looking for a BeatStars alternative?

If you sell beats on a marketplace like BeatStars today, you can try Creative Sound next to it. Setting up takes a few minutes: create a producer account, upload a track and choose your licenses.

Here, 80% of every sale is yours, the limits of each license are your choice, previews carry your own tag and every producer has a storefront at creativesound.io/@yourname. Buyers can browse the type beats and genres or the full catalogue.

Questions producers ask

How much do I earn per sale?

You keep 80% of every sale. Creative Sound keeps 20%, which covers card fees and hosting. There is no other cut on a sale.

When do I get paid?

Your first payout is held for 30 days, and after that each sale is held for 14 days: this is the refund and chargeback window, kept longer at the start for security. After a chargeback that is lost, the next payout is held for 30 days again. Then it is paid out on the next Monday, by PayPal or Stripe, once at least €10 is available. Anything under €10 waits and is added to the next payout.

Which licenses can I sell?

MP3, WAV, trackout stems and FL Studio project licenses. For each one you switch it on, set the price and choose the limits: streams, sales, videos, live shows, radio and your publishing share. You can add up to five short clauses of your own on top of a fixed legal base.

Do I keep the rights to my track?

Yes. You only license the track to the buyer under the terms you set. We can only sell finished tracks that you fully own, and you confirm that when you upload.

Is every track checked before it goes on sale?

Yes. We check the files and the details of every track and email you when it is approved, or tell you what to fix.

Can I sell on other marketplaces too?

Creative Sound does not ask you to stop selling elsewhere. If you also sell on a marketplace such as BeatStars, check the terms of every license you grant so the same rights are not promised twice.

How do buyers find my tracks?

Every approved track has its own page, your storefront lives at creativesound.io/@yourname, and tracks are listed on genre, mood, tag and type beat pages that search engines can read. Previews play right on those pages.

Can I offer a free download?

Yes, it is optional. You can offer a free tagged MP3 download on a track, and buyers get the clean files when they buy a license.

Ready to list your first track?

It takes a few minutes. We review every track and email you the result.
